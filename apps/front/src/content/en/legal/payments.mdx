# Payments and payment security

Online payments are processed in accordance with the rules of the international payment systems Visa, MasterCard and the Mir payment system,
following the principles of confidentiality and payment security, using modern methods of verification,
encryption and data transmission over secure channels.


## How to pay

When placing an order, choose the payment method that suits you.
After you click "Pay", you will be redirected to the payment gateway of one of the services listed above.
The connection to the payment gateway and the data transfer are secured with SSL encryption.
If your bank supports secure online payment technologies such as Verified By Visa, MasterCard SecureCode or MIR Accept,
you may also need to enter a special password to complete the payment.


On the card details page you will need to enter your bank card details:

* card number
* expiration date
* three-digit security code.

All of this is printed on the card itself. The three-digit security code is the three digits on the back of the card.

You will then be redirected to your bank's page to enter the 3DSecure code sent to you by SMS. If you don't receive the 3DSecure code, contact the bank that issued your card.


## PCI DSS standard

This is an international security standard for card payments: it includes 12 requirements
developed by Visa, Mastercard and other payment systems.

The payment systems listed above are certified as compliant with the PCI DSS standard for storing,
processing and transmitting cardholder data. The PCI DSS bank card security standard is supported by international
payment systems, including MasterCard and Visa, Inc.
Your confidential payment data (card details, registration data, etc.)
never reaches lania.network — it is processed by the processing centers and is fully protected.


## HTTPS protocol

HTTPS is a secure data transfer protocol that supports encryption with the SSL and TLS cryptographic protocols
and is an extended version of the HTTP protocol.

The connection between lania.network and the payment systems uses HTTPS: data is sent to the servers encrypted.
Information sent back is encrypted too.
