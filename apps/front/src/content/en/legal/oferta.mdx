## 1. Terms and definitions

- **Agreement** – the text of this Offer with its Appendices, which are an integral part of this Offer,
accepted by the Customer through the implied actions provided for by this Offer.

- **Implied actions** — conduct that expresses consent to a counterparty's proposal to conclude,
amend or terminate an agreement. Such actions consist of full or partial fulfillment of the terms proposed by the counterparty.

- **Contractor's website on the Internet** – a set of computer programs and other information
contained in an information system accessed via the Internet at the domain name
**lania.network**, including all its subdomains, game servers and sections (for example, the wiki, the personal account, etc.).

- **Parties to the Agreement (Parties)** – the Contractor and the Customer.

- **Service** – a service provided by the Contractor to the Customer in the manner and on the terms set out in this Offer.
