## Skins

<br/>
<Table>
  <thead>
    <Table.Tr>
      <Table.Th>Command</Table.Th>
      <Table.Th>Description</Table.Th>
    </Table.Tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <Table.Tr>
      <Table.Td style={{ whiteSpace: 'nowrap' }}>`/skins`</Table.Td>
      <Table.Td>Opens the skin menu, including a skin catalog.</Table.Td>
    </Table.Tr>
    <Table.Tr>
      <Table.Td style={{ whiteSpace: 'nowrap' }}>`/skin set {player}`</Table.Td>
      <Table.Td>Sets your skin to the skin of a licensed account by nickname.</Table.Td>
    </Table.Tr>
    <Table.Tr>
      <Table.Td style={{ whiteSpace: 'nowrap' }}>`/skin url {link}`</Table.Td>
      <Table.Td>
        Sets your skin from a link. The link must point to a `.png` file.
        Learn more in [this article](https://skinsrestorer.net/docs/features/skin-url).
      </Table.Td>
    </Table.Tr>
    <Table.Tr>
      <Table.Td style={{ whiteSpace: 'nowrap' }}>`/skin clear`</Table.Td>
      <Table.Td>Resets your skin to the default one (or to your licensed skin).</Table.Td>
    </Table.Tr>
    <Table.Tr>
      <Table.Td style={{ whiteSpace: 'nowrap' }}>`/skin update`</Table.Td>
      <Table.Td>Refreshes your skin. Useful if you changed the skin on your licensed account but it hasn't updated on the server.</Table.Td>
    </Table.Tr>
  </tbody>
</Table>


## Chat

<br/>
<Table>
  <thead>
    <Table.Tr>
      <Table.Th>Command</Table.Th>
      <Table.Th>Description</Table.Th>
    </Table.Tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <Table.Tr>
      <Table.Td style={{ whiteSpace: 'nowrap' }}>
        `/m {player} {message}`
      </Table.Td>
      <Table.Td>Sends a private message to another player.</Table.Td>
    </Table.Tr>
    <Table.Tr>
      <Table.Td style={{ whiteSpace: 'nowrap' }}>
        `/r`
      </Table.Td>
      <Table.Td>Replies to the last private message.</Table.Td>
    </Table.Tr>
    <Table.Tr>
      <Table.Td>
        `/translateto {source language} {target language} {message}`
      </Table.Td>
      <Table.Td>
        Translates a message into the target language and sends it to chat.

        Example: `/translateto ru en Привет, как дела?` → `Hello, how are you?`

        [Available language codes](https://localizely.com/iso-639-1-list/).
      </Table.Td>
    </Table.Tr>
    <Table.Tr>
      <Table.Td style={{ whiteSpace: 'nowrap' }}>
        `/ignore {player}`
      </Table.Td>
      <Table.Td>Ignores all messages from a player (run the command again to stop ignoring them).</Table.Td>
    </Table.Tr>
    <Table.Tr>
      <Table.Td style={{ whiteSpace: 'nowrap' }}>
        `/ignorelist`
      </Table.Td>
      <Table.Td>Shows the players you are ignoring.</Table.Td>
    </Table.Tr>
    <Table.Tr>
      <Table.Td style={{ whiteSpace: 'nowrap' }}>
        `/mail {player} {message}`
      </Table.Td>
      <Table.Td>Sends mail to a player who is offline. They will see it when they join the server.</Table.Td>
    </Table.Tr>
    <Table.Tr>
      <Table.Td style={{ whiteSpace: 'nowrap' }}>
        `/clearmail {id}`
      </Table.Td>
      <Table.Td>Deletes the sent mail with the given id.</Table.Td>
    </Table.Tr>
    <Table.Tr>
      <Table.Td style={{ whiteSpace: 'nowrap' }}>
        `/do`
      </Table.Td>
      <Table.Td>Styles the message as a roleplay action.</Table.Td>
    </Table.Tr>
    <Table.Tr>
      <Table.Td style={{ whiteSpace: 'nowrap' }}>
        `/me`
      </Table.Td>
      <Table.Td>Styles the message as a roleplay message.</Table.Td>
    </Table.Tr>
  </tbody>
</Table>

<Table>
  <thead>
