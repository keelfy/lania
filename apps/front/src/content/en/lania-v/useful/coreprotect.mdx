# Block history — CoreProtect

On Lania, **`/co i`** is available to every player. It enables the CoreProtect inspector so you can see who changed or interacted with blocks and when. No client mod is needed.

## How to inspect history

1. Enter `/co i` in chat. The full command is `/co inspect`.
2. **Left-click a block** to inspect the history at its position.
3. To check a missing block, **right-click the face of a neighbouring block** beside the position it occupied.
4. **Right-click a chest, door, button or lever** to inspect recorded interactions.
5. Read the chat records for the player, action and time.
6. Enter `/co i` again to disable the inspector and resume normal block interactions.

## If you find a problem

The inspector shows recorded history. When checking a chest, consider both interactions and changes to its contents, if those are logged.

If items are missing or a build is damaged, save the coordinates and a screenshot of the records. Contact the [server staff](/en/wiki/lania-v/useful/staff) or use `/report`.

Inspector mode reads history. Contact staff for block restoration.
