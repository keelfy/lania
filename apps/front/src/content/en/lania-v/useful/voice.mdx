# Voice chat

The server runs the [Plasmo Voice](https://modrinth.com/mod/plasmo-voice) voice chat: you hear nearby players in 3D, and voices fade with distance.

<Callout type='info'>
  You can play without the mod, but voice chat won't be available.
  [keelfy's modpack](/en/wiki/lania-v/useful/modpack) already includes Plasmo Voice and all client-side add-ons.
</Callout>

## Installation

1. Install the [Fabric](https://fabricmc.net/use) mod loader (or NeoForge);
2. Download [Plasmo Voice](https://modrinth.com/mod/plasmo-voice) for your game version;
3. Put the mod into the `mods` folder in your game directory;
4. Join the server and press `V`: check that the right microphone and headphones are selected.

If your microphone isn't detected, see the [developers' guide](https://plasmovoice.com/docs/client/microphone-not-available/).

In the Activation tab of the `V` menu you pick a mode for each way of talking: **Push to Talk** (talk on a key) or **Voice Activation** (the microphone turns on when you speak). Your normal voice distance is set there too.

## Add-ons

Add-ons add new ways to talk. Most of them show up as separate activations in the Activation tab of the `V` menu &mdash; give them a key or a mode, like your normal voice.

<Callout type='warning'>
  Access to some add-ons depends on your permissions. If an activation isn't in the menu or a command says you lack permission, that feature isn't available to you.
</Callout>

### Groups

[pv-addon-groups](https://modrinth.com/plugin/pv-addon-groups) &mdash; voice channels for your friends: group members hear each other at any distance.

1. Set up the Groups activation in the `V` menu;
2. Create a group: `/groups create`;
3. Invite friends: `/groups invite {player}`;
4. Talk using the group activation.

You can set a name and a password when creating it, for example: `/groups create name: Miners password: qwerty`. Both are optional and their order doesn't matter. A group is deleted automatically when it's empty and when the server restarts.

### Whisper

[pv-addon-whisper](https://modrinth.com/plugin/pv-addon-whisper) &mdash; the Whisper activation: you're heard at a shorter distance than usual (half by default). Handy when you don't want to bother your neighbours.

### Priority

[pv-addon-priority](https://modrinth.com/plugin/pv-addon-priority) &mdash; the Priority activation: a loud voice that carries farther than usual.

### Broadcast

[pv-addon-broadcast](https://modrinth.com/plugin/pv-addon-broadcast) &mdash; the Broadcast activation: every player in the chosen area hears you. Used for events and announcements.

Set the area with `/vbc`:

- `/vbc range {radius}` &mdash; players within a radius;
- `/vbc world {world}` &mdash; the whole world;
- `/vbc server` &mdash; the whole server;
- `/vbcp proxy` &mdash; every server in the network.

### Spectators

[pv-addon-spectator](https://modrinth.com/plugin/pv-addon-spectator) &mdash; everyone can hear players in spectator mode.

### Sculk

[pv-addon-sculk](https://modrinth.com/plugin/pv-addon-sculk) &mdash; sculk sensors and wardens react to your voice. Talking loudly next to a sensor emits a redstone signal, and in an Ancient City it can attract the warden. Speak quietly or use groups.

### Music discs

[pv-addon-discs](https://modrinth.com/plugin/pv-addon-discs) &mdash; record your own music onto a disc. It plays from a jukebox, and everyone nearby hears it.

1. Hold any music disc;
2. Type `/disc burn {link} [name]`;
3. Put the disc into a jukebox.

Supported links: YouTube (including live streams), SoundCloud, Bandcamp, Vimeo, Twitch and direct links to audio files (`.mp3`, `.flac`, `.wav`, `.ogg`, `.m4a` and more).

### Client-side add-ons

These add-ons go into the `mods` folder and only work for you. The modpack already has them.

- [pv-addon-soundphysics](https://modrinth.com/mod/pv-addon-soundphysics) &mdash; voices follow [Sound Physics Remastered](https://modrinth.com/mod/sound-physics-remastered): echo in caves, muffling behind walls;
- [pv-addon-replaymod](https://modrinth.com/mod/pv-addon-replaymod) &mdash; voice chat is recorded into [ReplayMod](https://www.replaymod.com/) replays;
- [pv-addon-flashback](https://modrinth.com/mod/pv-addon-flashback) &mdash; the same for [Flashback](https://modrinth.com/mod/flashback) replays.

## Common problems

- **Nobody hears me** &mdash; check in the `V` menu that the right microphone is selected and it isn't muted with `M`. In Voice Activation mode, adjust the sensitivity threshold;
- **Voice chat disconnected** &mdash; type `/vrc`;
- **A player is too loud** &mdash; hold RMB while looking at them and lower their volume with the mouse wheel;
- **A player breaks the rules in voice chat** &mdash; report them with `/report`.
