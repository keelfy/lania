# Petting — PatPat

PatPat lets you pet players and living mobs. Lania has the server plugin, which shares petting actions with other players using the mod.

## What to install

You need the [PatPat client mod](https://modrinth.com/mod/patpat). It is included in [keelfy's modpack](/en/wiki/lania-v/useful/modpack).

The plugin does not replace the client mod: other players also need PatPat to see the petting animations.

## How to pet

Approach a player or mob, aim at them and press **Shift + right-click**.
