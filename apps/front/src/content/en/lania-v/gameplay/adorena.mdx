# Character size — Adorena

Adorena makes your character grow when you get kills. On Lania, this is a server mechanic: no client mod is needed.

## How to grow

Kill mobs or players — killing any creature increases your character's size. Growth accumulates up to **5% above your normal size**.

## Returning to normal size

- **Drink milk** to remove accumulated growth.
- **Any death** also clears all growth, regardless of its cause.

After a reset, you return to your normal size. New kills make your character grow again.
