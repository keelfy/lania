# New advancements

The survival worlds (Survival, Farms) have the [BlazeandCave's Advancements](https://modrinth.com/datapack/blazeandcaves-advancements-pack) datapack, which adds over a thousand new advancements.

## Advancement translations

You can download a resource pack that translates the advancements into other languages.

[Bacap Language Pack](https://modrinth.com/resourcepack/bacap-language-pack)

### Installation

[keelfy's modpack](/en/wiki/lania-v/useful/modpack) already includes the pack &mdash; just enable it in the resource pack settings. Without the modpack:

1. Put the resource pack into the `resourcepacks` folder in your game directory.
2. Launch the game.
3. Enable the resource pack in the game settings.
