# Pings — Ping Wheel

Ping Wheel lets you temporarily mark locations, blocks and entities for co-op play. Lania has the server plugin that shares pings.

## What to install

Sending and viewing pings requires the [Ping Wheel client mod](https://modrinth.com/mod/ping-wheel). It is included in [keelfy's modpack](/en/wiki/lania-v/useful/modpack). Other players also need the mod to see your pings.

## How to ping

1. Aim at a location, block or entity.
2. Press **Mouse5**, the mouse's side “Forward” button by default.
3. Hold **Tab** to see who placed each ping.

If your mouse does not have this button, assign another key in Minecraft's controls.

## Settings

Open `/pingwheel config` or Ping Wheel's settings in Mod Menu to adjust ping visuals and sound.

Pings are temporary and do not replace permanent map waypoints.
