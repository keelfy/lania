# Loot container markers

Lania uses the [JustLootIt](https://modrinth.com/mod/justlootit) plugin: every player gets their own loot in the chests and other containers generated by the world. Taking your loot does not take anything from others, and theirs does not take anything from you.

Because of this, a container with loot looks like any other one. The **Lania Loot Marker** client mod marks such containers and shows whether you have already opened them.

## What the mod does

- Particles appear around a loot container. **Gold** means you can loot it, **gray-blue** means you have already taken its loot.
- A loot container, including both halves of a double chest, breaks only while you hold **Shift**. Without Shift the hit is cancelled and a hint appears above the hotbar.
- Markers are per player: they depend only on what you have opened.

The mod works together with the Lania server plugin. On other servers it does nothing, and without the mod the game on Lania stays the same: you just don't get the markers and the hint.

## Installation

The mod is already included in [keelfy's modpack](/en/wiki/lania-v/useful/modpack). To install it separately, you need Minecraft 26.3, [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/) and [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api).

[Download lania-loot-marker-1.0.0.jar](/downloads/lania-loot-marker-1.0.0.jar) — put the file into the `mods` folder.

## If a marker did not change

After you take the loot, the marker changes color right away. If the loot was restored by a JustLootIt refresh timer, the marker updates later, when the chunk is sent to you again, for example after you re-enter the area.
