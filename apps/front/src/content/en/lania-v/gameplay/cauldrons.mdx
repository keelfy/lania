# Cauldrons

On Lania servers cauldrons can do more than in vanilla.

## Hardening concrete

Right-click a water cauldron while holding concrete powder to turn it into concrete. Each click converts one block and uses one level of water.

![Hardening concrete](https://czx1jtlf2o.ufs.sh/f/0UHiIrRo6i8c2HT8BBGDOK4vrPw6ExgjZ95TMiUdkAqb1QNo "Hardening concrete")

## Mud

Right-click a water cauldron while holding dirt to turn it into mud. Each click converts one block and uses one level of water.

## Potions

- Right-click an **empty** cauldron with a potion to pour it in;
- Right-click a potion cauldron with an empty bottle to fill it back;
- Right-click a potion cauldron with arrows to get tipped arrows with that potion's effect.

![Potions](https://czx1jtlf2o.ufs.sh/f/0UHiIrRo6i8ccVOHQEy5g7AOZNh68Dkvu4BQxKFmLJrVjqU3 "Potions")

## Copper

There are two special cauldrons for copper items. To fill one, right-click an empty cauldron with slime or honey blocks: `3` blocks fill it completely.

- **Slime cauldron** — right-click it with a copper block to oxidize (age) the block;
- **Honey cauldron** — right-click it with a copper block to wax the block.

Each click converts one block.
