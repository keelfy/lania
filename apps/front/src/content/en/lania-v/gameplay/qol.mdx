# Quality of life

## Harvesting with right-click

You can harvest crops (wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroots) with right-click. Seeds are replanted automatically after harvesting.

You can toggle this with the `/scythe toggle` command.

## Skipping the night

You don't need every player asleep to skip the night. `30%` of players sleeping is enough.

## Choosing a painting

You can pick a specific painting in the stonecutter instead of trying to "catch" the one you want while placing it.

![Stonecutter](https://czx1jtlf2o.ufs.sh/f/0UHiIrRo6i8cggXcDvwxuMGBHO5h9K3TU7ovPs4beatINCZA)

## Sawmill

The stonecutter can make wooden items (stairs, slabs, fences, doors, signs, etc.) from planks, logs and wood.

![Stonecutter](https://czx1jtlf2o.ufs.sh/f/0UHiIrRo6i8cvknNCMdiFz6ZLKxUEBu1poQVlRHge9YT0j8q)

## Fast leaf decay

After you chop down a tree, its leaves decay in a few seconds instead of minutes.

## Instant deepslate mining

Deepslate and its blocks (bricks, tiles, polished and chiseled deepslate) break instantly if you have a netherite tool with Efficiency V and Haste II or higher.

## Grass from dirt

Right-click a dirt block with seeds for a chance to turn it into a grass block: `30%` with wheat seeds and `3%` with melon, pumpkin and beetroot seeds.

## All recipes unlocked

All recipes in the recipe book are unlocked as soon as you start playing.
