# Home

A home is a point you can return to with one command. Lania uses the [Simple Homes](https://modrinth.com/plugin/simple-homes) plugin: you save a home and teleport to it.

<Callout type='info'>
  Home commands are not part of the basic set. They are unlocked by a separate one-season privilege that you can buy in the [store](/en/products) on the "Privileges" tab.
</Callout>

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/sethome` | Saves a home at your current position. If you already have one, moves it here |
| `/home` | Teleports you to your home |
| `/delhome` | Deletes your home |

Each player has one home, so you do not need to give it a name. The home is separate in each season.

## Safe teleportation

The plugin checks the destination so you do not end up in lava or inside a block. If the place is not safe, the teleport does not happen.

## How to get it

1. Open the [store](/en/products) and go to the "Privileges" tab.
2. Add the home privilege to your basket and pay for the order.
3. The privilege works in the season you bought it for and lasts until the end of that season.

If the commands did not appear after payment, open your profile on the site and press "Synchronize".
