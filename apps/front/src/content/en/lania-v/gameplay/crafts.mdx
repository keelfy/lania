# Custom crafts

The server has recipes that don't exist in vanilla Minecraft.

## End Rod

You can craft an End Rod on a regular crafting table without visiting the End. One craft yields `4` rods.

<CraftingRecipe
  lang="en"
  shape={[' G ', 'GSG', 'PBP']}
  ingredients={{
    G: 'glowstone_dust',
    S: 'stick',
    P: 'purple_dye',
    B: 'brick',
  }}
  result="end_rod"
  amount={4}
/>

- `G` — Glowstone Dust;
- `S` — Stick;
- `P` — Purple Dye;
- `B` — Brick.

The recipe is shaped: place the items exactly as shown in the diagram.
