# Nether portals

## Portals of any shape

Nether portals can be any shape and size — the frame doesn't have to be a rectangle. The inside of the portal must be at least `6` blocks (like `2×3`) and at most `23×23`. Besides obsidian, you can use crying obsidian in the frame.

![Round portal](https://czx1jtlf2o.ufs.sh/f/0UHiIrRo6i8chemNkVbsnNdZiPcVEyKzarAw7UT6p3DCHRBS "Round portal")

## Calculating portal coordinates

The `/portal` command calculates where to build a portal in the other dimension so it links to the portal near you.
