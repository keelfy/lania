# Mobs

Some mobs behave differently on Lania servers.

## Endermen

Endermen don't pick up blocks, so your builds and the terrain around them stay intact.

## Confetti creepers

A creeper has a `5%` chance to explode into confetti: the explosion doesn't break blocks and deals half the damage.

## Phantom membranes

Phantoms are disabled on the server, so phantom membranes drop from bats instead.

## Husks

Husks (desert zombies) drop `0–2` sand on death, which makes sand renewable. Looting raises the maximum by the enchantment level.

## Silent mobs

To make a mob stop making sounds, rename it with a name tag to `Silence Me` (`Silence me`, `silence me` and `silence_me` work too).

![Silent cow](https://czx1jtlf2o.ufs.sh/f/0UHiIrRo6i8cLLKu1BjEsC3fShGRdVcyo051XAQxHumeM2OJ "Silent cow")

## Transferring pets

To give a tamed pet to another player, put it on a lead and right-click the player you want to give it to.

## Golden dandelion

Right-click a mob with a golden dandelion:

- an adult mob turns back into a baby;
- a baby grows up instantly.

It also works on villagers and all kinds of zombies.
