# Hiding armor — Armor Hider

Armor Hider lets you hide your armor, offhand item and enchantment glint, or make them translucent. Lania has a server plugin that shares your settings with other players.

## What to install

You need the [Armor Hider client mod](https://modrinth.com/mod/zannaghs-armor-hider). It is included in [keelfy's modpack](/en/wiki/lania-v/useful/modpack). Other players also need the mod to see your armor hidden; without it they see your armor as usual.

## How to hide armor

1. Press **J** to open Armor Hider's settings.
2. Adjust the opacity of your helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots and offhand item.
3. Optionally turn off enchantment glint for specific slots.

Press **K** to quickly toggle hiding on and off. You can rebind both keys in Minecraft's controls.

The settings also let you save presets and automatically show your armor in combat.
