# Vein mining — VeinMiner

VeinMiner mines connected blocks in a vein when you break one of them. It works on ores (pickaxe) and on wood (axe). On Lania, this mechanic requires a tool with the **Veinmine** enchantment. The server uses VeinMiner's default settings.

## Getting the enchantment

Enchant a pickaxe or an axe at an enchanting table and look for **Veinmine** among the available enchantments. It appears randomly, so it may take several attempts.

## How to use it

1. Hold a pickaxe or an axe with Veinmine. A pickaxe works on ore, an axe works on wood.
2. Break one block in the vein or one block of the trunk. Connected blocks are mined automatically.

You do not need to sneak or press a separate key. No client mod is required.

## Limits

- Without the enchantment, your pickaxe and axe mine normally.
- The default rules target ores with pickaxes and wood with axes: the enchantment does not enable mass mining of every block type.
- The maximum chain is **100 blocks**.
- Every mined block consumes tool durability.
- Your tool must be suitable for the blocks you mine: the enchantment does not bypass tool requirements.
