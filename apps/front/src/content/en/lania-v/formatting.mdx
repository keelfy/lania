# Formatting

This section explains how you can format your chat messages.

## Text

You can use the following tags to format text in chat:

- `**text**` → **text**
- `*text*` → *text*
- `~~text~~` → ~~text~~
- `__text__` → <u>text</u>
- `||he dies at the end of the stream||` → becomes hidden text that is only shown when you hover over it in chat.

## Mentioning players

You can mention players in chat by writing their nickname with the `@` symbol. Example: `@keelfy`.

The mentioned player hears a sound notification and their nickname is highlighted in chat.

## Links

Links sent to chat that start with a protocol (`http` or `https`) automatically become clickable.

- `https://lania.network` → [lania.network](https://lania.network)

## Placeholders

You can send special codes to chat, which are shown as their actual value.

- `%ping%` → Ping of the player who sent the message
- `%tps%` → Current server TPS
- `%online%` → Number of players online
- `%coords%` → Coordinates of the player who sent the message
- `%stats%` → Health, food, armor and other stats of the player who sent the message
- `%skin%` → Skin of the player who sent the message
- `%item%` → Item held by the player who sent the message
